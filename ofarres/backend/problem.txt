I am doing an entitiy linking problem which combines a dual architecture:
1-NER
2-RAG

The goal is to, given N notes of ictus pacients, we extract the K medical entities coded with the SNOMED-CT terminology. 

Now I am focusing on the NER part. I now the errors are a cascade, comitting an error in the NER part will just be multiplied in the RAG part. The idea is to get a perfect 100% f1-score. However this is very difficult. That's why I am focusing on a 100% recall and then trying to have an almost 90% precision. To get a 100% recall I am using a hybrid approach:

As I need an ontology for the RAG, I must as well use it to boost my recall.
1-BERTs from scispaCy.
2-DFA RegEx from the ontology (O(n) time).

---

After boosting the NER recall to 1.00 (100%), it is time to implementte the NER judge, to boost the precision to ~95%
I was wondering what strategies could I use. I was thinking of using an LLM to judge the NER results, but I don't know if this is a good idea.
I also thought of using a Bi-Encoder.
